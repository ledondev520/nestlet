# Five-case pilot protocol

**Status: planned, not run. No measured result is claimed.** This protocol measures operator labor and output quality, not agency processing time, approval probability or market demand.

## Preparation

Select one PHA, one program variant and one recurring administrative task. Use five synthetic or explicitly authorized, thoroughly de-identified historical cases. Freeze historical inputs at the earlier knowledge state; do not leak later outcomes into the task. Do not ingest tax, banking, identity, medical or household records.

An experienced operator prepares an answer key: supported facts, unknowns, critical conflicts, acceptable next actions, forbidden claims and current official references. Current implementation covers five administrative fields and generic guidance only; evaluate additional packet checks manually, never assume they were automated.

## Case mix

1. Straightforward complete information: avoid unnecessary requests
2. Missing field/signature evidence: ask precisely without claiming agency nonreceipt
3. Conflicting rent, unit or utility evidence: retain uncertainty rather than silently choose
4. New owner or agent-authority ambiguity: preserve role limits
5. Pending agency reply or ambiguous inspection status: ask for status without inventing approval or dates

## Comparison

Compare folders/spreadsheet/email work against assisted work for the same output. Prefer two operators with counterbalanced order across cases and no answer-sharing. With one operator, use matched variants where feasible and explicitly report repeat-case learning bias. Five cases support descriptive findings, not a statistical causal claim.

Start timing when the operator opens the task. Stop at a human-reviewed usable draft. Include reading, input/import, fact review, corrections, drafting, export and final verification. Record onboarding separately; include attributable setup in assisted cost. Separate active handling, tool waiting and agency elapsed time. Send nothing during the test.

Use a consistent total-cost definition for both workflows and record waiting separately as well. Net minutes saved = manual total minus assisted total. Report all five paired outcomes, median and range, including negative results. Percentage saved = net divided by manual total; omit it if the manual denominator is zero.

Record unsupported statements, missed critical facts, false missing-item requests, wrongly resolved conflicts, revision rounds and reviewer usability. Flag critical errors separately: invented people/rent/deadlines, false eligibility/approval, unauthorized signatures, or inappropriate sensitive-data requests. Preserve original and corrected drafts so review costs and safety defects are not hidden.

## Decision gate agreed before testing

The operator must choose what saving justifies reuse. A proposed gate is no critical error in final reviewed outputs, no quality regression relative to manual work, and repeatable net savings that exceed setup and review cost. This is a product proposal, not an industry benchmark. If review erases the gain, simplify or stop; if agency waiting dominates, do not claim drafting solves it.

Only after benefit is observed ask about normal volume, existing portal coverage, willingness to reuse and who would pay. Do not invent annual ROI, five-case results or customer testimony.
